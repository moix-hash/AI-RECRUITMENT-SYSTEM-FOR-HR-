# 5–10 minute screen-recording checklist

Record one continuous demonstration covering the following required items:

1. **Problem statement** — explain the time required for manual CV screening and the TalentOS solution.
2. **Dataset** — open `assets/samples/` and `dataset/`, explain that data is synthetic demo content, and show the CV/JD fields.
3. **Model/algorithm** — explain PDF extraction, recognised-skill matching, score calculation, local fallback, and optional Gemini/LangChain analysis. State that no custom model was trained.
4. **Code overview** — show `app/`, `pages/`, `services/`, `utils/analysis_fallback.py`, `database/`, `tests/`, and `requirements.txt`.
5. **Live execution** — run the app, create/upload a job description, upload a resume, show extracted text and the readiness report, then show candidate ranking and pipeline/interview scheduling.
6. **Results and conclusion** — show score, skill match/gaps, recommendation, interview questions, and explain the value plus limitations.

Use `docs/demo-video-script.md` for a timed 10-minute narration. Save the recording in the submission portal or include its accessible link as instructed by your teacher.
